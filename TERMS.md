## Where your data goes

The application runs entirely in your browser. Almost nothing leaves the device.
There are exactly **four** points at which anything is sent anywhere, and this is
all of them:

| When | Where to | What is sent |
|---|---|---|
| Every time the app loads | Google Fonts (`fonts.googleapis.com`, `fonts.gstatic.com`) | Your IP address and browser identification, as with any web font. No progress, no account details. Once installed to a home screen the fonts are cached and this stops. |
| Only if cloud sync is switched on | Your Supabase project | A first name, an identifier derived from a PIN, and practice state — words learned, XP, streak, coins, items owned, lesson progress. Never the PIN itself. |
| Every time the app loads | GitHub Pages, the host | Your IP address and the file requested, in ordinary web server logs kept by GitHub. |
| Only while you use Speaking practice | Your browser's speech recognition service | A recording of your voice. **In Chrome and Edge this is sent to Google's servers to be transcribed.** Safari on Apple devices may process it on-device. This is the browser's own facility, not ours, and nothing is stored by this application. Speaking practice is optional; every other part of the app works without the microphone. |

Everything else — every word you learn, every answer, every mistake, the room you
decorate, your streak — is stored in your own browser and goes nowhere unless cloud
sync is on.

There is no analytics, no tracking pixel, no advertising, no third-party script of
any kind. There is nothing to opt out of, because there is nothing collecting.

## Accounts, if you make one

An account is a first name and a PIN of your choosing. The PIN is never stored or
transmitted; an identifier derived from it is. That means two things worth knowing:

- **A forgotten PIN cannot be recovered**, by you or by us. There is no reset,
  because there is nothing to reset against.
- Anyone who knows both the name and the PIN can reach that progress. Do not use a
  PIN you use for anything that matters.

Use a first name, a nickname, or anything you like. Nothing checks, and nothing else
about you is asked for or wanted.
